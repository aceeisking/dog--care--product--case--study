


# Case Study: Dog Care Management App (MVP)

**Role:** Lead Product Manager (Discovery, Wireframing, Scope Definition, PRD Authoring)  
**Tools Used:** Figma, FigJam, Notion, Markdown  
**Target Audience:** First-time and busy dog owners  
**Project Status:** Completed MVP Specs & User Testing  

---

## 1. Project Overview & Background
Pet ownership—especially for first-time dog owners—comes with high mental load. Owners frequently struggle to keep track of daily care routines (feeding, grooming, potty schedules), monitor health symptoms, and make quick, reliable decisions during urgent situations. 

Existing solutions are either bloated with unnecessary social features or too complex for quick daily updates. This project focuses on building a **streamlined mobile-first solution** that gives dog owners fast access to essential care tracking and immediate guidance.

---

## 2. Problem Statement & User Insights

### The Problem
Dog owners experience high decision fatigue and friction when managing daily pet care schedules and identifying health or behavioral concerns. Key pain points include:
* **Information Overload:** Searching for pet advice online leads to conflicting, overwhelming results.
* **Scattered Tracking:** Routine care tasks (medication, vet visits, feeding) are tracked in disparate places or forgotten.
* **Urgency Friction:** In urgent or stressful situations, owners need clear, low-friction answers instantly without scrolling through dense text.

### Target User Personas
1. **The First-Time Owner:** Needs clear routines, reminders, and trusted guidance to build confidence.
2. **The Busy Pet Parent:** Needs a 10-second daily check-in tool to track feeding, potty times, and care duties without distraction.

---

## 4. Key Strategic Trade-Offs

| Decision Area | What Was Prioritized | What Was Deprioritized | Product Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Information Architecture** | **Clarity & Speed** | Exhaustive Detail | In stressful or routine moments, dense text causes drop-off. Surfacing top 2–3 actionable steps reduces decision fatigue. |
| **Onboarding Flow** | **Progressive Disclosure** | Heavy Initial Setup | Deferred non-essential profile fields to secondary settings to maximize core activation speed on day one. |
| **Trust & Content** | **Verified Direct Guidance** | Open Forums | Prioritized curated, structured care guidance over crowd-sourced advice to avoid misleading or conflicting information. |

---

## 5. User Validation & Iteration Loop

To validate concepts before development, low-fidelity wireframes were tested directly with dog owners:

* **Testing Focus:** Evaluated task-completion speed, screen readability during urgent situations, and navigation clarity.
* **User Feedback:** Users preferred clean visual checklists over long text descriptions and wanted critical actions accessible within two taps from the home screen.
* **Key Iterations:**
  1. Reduced onboarding steps from 6 screens down to 3 required fields.
  2. Moved the "Urgent Care / Symptom Checker" button to a fixed primary dock on the home screen.
  3. Redesigned the daily task tracker into simple tap-to-complete cards.

---

## 6. Success Metrics & KPIs

To measure feature success post-launch, performance is divided into engagement and retention metrics:

* **North Star Metric:** **Weekly Active Care Finishers (WACF)** — Percentage of active users who complete at least 4 daily care routines per week.
* **Activation Metric:** Percentage of new users who complete pet onboarding and set their first reminder on Day 1 (Target: >65%).
* **Retention Metric:** 30-Day User Retention Rate (Target: >35%).
* **Counter Metric:** Support ticket volume / negative feedback regarding information clarity (Target: <2% of active users).

---

## 7. Product Learnings & Next Steps

1. **Speed to Value is Essential:** For daily pet management, minimizing screen time and taps directly correlates with product adoption and long-term user retention.
2. **Next Steps for Phase 2:** Conduct usability testing on high-fidelity prototypes and prepare product backlog for sprint planning.
